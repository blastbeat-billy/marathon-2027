Why I'm doing this

I've run two marathons before, both trail marathons, including a 50K ultra, but never a flat road marathon and nothing since 2024. This one's for my kids' school, which has asked runners to raise a minimum of £2,500.

I haven't run consistently in a while, so this training block starts from a genuine rebuild: getting back to comfortable long-run fitness first, then working down toward race pace over the following months. What's on this page is the real week-by-week evidence of that, pulled straight from Strava.
